# Paediatrics department

## About the department

The Paediatrics department cares for babies, children and teenagers up to 18 years, including vaccinations and growth check-ups.

## Common reasons to visit

- fever, cough and cold in children
- vaccinations
- growth and nutrition check-ups
- feeding problems in babies
- school fitness certificates

## OPD timings and location

The Paediatrics OPD is on the first floor. OPD hours are 10:00–14:00 on each doctor's OPD days (see below). Sunday is closed except for emergencies.

## Doctors

- Dr. Aparna Deshpande: OPD on Monday, Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday and Friday; speaks Marathi and English.
- Dr. Joseph Mathew: OPD on Tuesday, Thursday and Saturday; speaks English.

## Before your visit

- Bring your previous prescriptions, reports and a photo ID.
- Arrive 15 minutes before your appointment and check in at the reception or with CareFlow.
- Note down your symptoms and questions so you do not forget them during the consultation.
