# Ophthalmology (eye care) department

## About the department

The Ophthalmology department checks vision and treats eye conditions; it also prescribes glasses.

## Common reasons to visit

- blurred vision and checks for glasses
- red, watery or itchy eyes
- cataract assessment
- eye check-ups for diabetes
- eye pressure (glaucoma) checks

## OPD timings and location

The Ophthalmology (eye care) OPD is on the fourth floor. OPD hours are 09:00–13:00 on each doctor's OPD days (see below). Sunday is closed except for emergencies.

## Doctors

- Dr. Nitin Chavan: OPD on Monday, Wednesday and Friday; speaks Marathi, Hindi and English.
- Dr. Lakshmi Rao: OPD on Tuesday, Thursday and Saturday; speaks English.

## Before your visit

- Bring your previous prescriptions, reports and a photo ID.
- Arrive 15 minutes before your appointment and check in at the reception or with CareFlow.
- Note down your symptoms and questions so you do not forget them during the consultation.
