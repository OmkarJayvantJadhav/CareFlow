# Gynaecology department

## About the department

The Gynaecology department provides care for women's health, including pregnancy check-ups, menstrual problems and menopause.

## Common reasons to visit

- pregnancy check-ups
- irregular or painful periods
- menopause problems
- family planning advice
- routine screening such as Pap smear

## OPD timings and location

The Gynaecology OPD is on the third floor. OPD hours are 09:30–13:30 on each doctor's OPD days (see below). Sunday is closed except for emergencies.

## Doctors

- Dr. Sunita Patwardhan: OPD on Monday, Tuesday, Thursday and Friday; speaks Marathi, English and Hindi.
- Dr. Ayesha Khan: OPD on Wednesday and Saturday; speaks Hindi and English.

## Before your visit

- Bring your previous prescriptions, reports and a photo ID.
- Arrive 15 minutes before your appointment and check in at the reception or with CareFlow.
- Note down your symptoms and questions so you do not forget them during the consultation.
