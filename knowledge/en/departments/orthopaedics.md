# Orthopaedics department

## About the department

The Orthopaedics department treats bones, joints, muscles, the spine and sports injuries.

## Common reasons to visit

- knee, hip or shoulder pain
- back and neck pain
- sprains and sports injuries
- follow-up of fractures
- arthritis and osteoporosis

## OPD timings and location

The Orthopaedics OPD is on the second floor. OPD hours are 09:30–13:30 on each doctor's OPD days (see below). Sunday is closed except for emergencies.

## Doctors

- Dr. Vikram Bhosale: OPD on Monday, Wednesday, Friday and Saturday; speaks Marathi, Hindi and English.
- Dr. Neha Kapoor: OPD on Tuesday and Thursday; speaks English and Hindi.

## Before your visit

- Bring your previous prescriptions, reports and a photo ID.
- Arrive 15 minutes before your appointment and check in at the reception or with CareFlow.
- Note down your symptoms and questions so you do not forget them during the consultation.
