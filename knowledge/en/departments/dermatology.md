# Dermatology department

## About the department

The Dermatology department treats conditions of the skin, hair and nails.

## Common reasons to visit

- acne and pimples
- itching, rashes and allergies of the skin
- hair fall and dandruff
- fungal infections
- pigmentation and white patches

## OPD timings and location

The Dermatology OPD is on the fourth floor. OPD hours are 09:00–13:00 on each doctor's OPD days (see below). Sunday is closed except for emergencies.

## Doctors

- Dr. Ritu Agarwal: OPD on Monday, Wednesday and Friday; speaks Hindi and English.
- Dr. Prakash Shinde: OPD on Tuesday and Saturday; speaks Marathi and Hindi.

## Before your visit

- Bring your previous prescriptions, reports and a photo ID.
- Arrive 15 minutes before your appointment and check in at the reception or with CareFlow.
- Note down your symptoms and questions so you do not forget them during the consultation.
