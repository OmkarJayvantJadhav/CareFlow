# Dr. Nitin Chavan, Ophthalmology (eye care)

## Profile

Dr. Nitin Chavan is a consultant in the Ophthalmology (eye care) department (fourth floor). OPD days: Monday, Wednesday and Friday, 09:00–13:00. Languages: Marathi, Hindi and English. Each appointment slot is 10 minutes.

## Areas of interest

cataract and diabetic eye disease.

## How to book

Book, reschedule or cancel an appointment by chatting with CareFlow in English, Hindi or Marathi, or at the reception desk on the ground floor. Walk-in patients get a token at the reception and are seen between booked appointments.
