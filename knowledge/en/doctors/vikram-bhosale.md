# Dr. Vikram Bhosale, Orthopaedics

## Profile

Dr. Vikram Bhosale is a consultant in the Orthopaedics department (second floor). OPD days: Monday, Wednesday, Friday and Saturday, 09:30–13:30. Languages: Marathi, Hindi and English. Each appointment slot is 15 minutes.

## Areas of interest

knee and hip problems and sports injuries.

## How to book

Book, reschedule or cancel an appointment by chatting with CareFlow in English, Hindi or Marathi, or at the reception desk on the ground floor. Walk-in patients get a token at the reception and are seen between booked appointments.
