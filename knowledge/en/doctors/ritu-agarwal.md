# Dr. Ritu Agarwal, Dermatology

## Profile

Dr. Ritu Agarwal is a consultant in the Dermatology department (fourth floor). OPD days: Monday, Wednesday and Friday, 09:00–13:00. Languages: Hindi and English. Each appointment slot is 15 minutes.

## Areas of interest

acne, hair fall and skin allergies.

## How to book

Book, reschedule or cancel an appointment by chatting with CareFlow in English, Hindi or Marathi, or at the reception desk on the ground floor. Walk-in patients get a token at the reception and are seen between booked appointments.
