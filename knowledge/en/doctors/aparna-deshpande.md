# Dr. Aparna Deshpande, Paediatrics

## Profile

Dr. Aparna Deshpande is a consultant in the Paediatrics department (first floor). OPD days: Monday, Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday and Friday, 10:00–14:00. Languages: Marathi and English. Each appointment slot is 15 minutes.

## Areas of interest

newborn care, vaccinations and child nutrition.

## How to book

Book, reschedule or cancel an appointment by chatting with CareFlow in English, Hindi or Marathi, or at the reception desk on the ground floor. Walk-in patients get a token at the reception and are seen between booked appointments.
