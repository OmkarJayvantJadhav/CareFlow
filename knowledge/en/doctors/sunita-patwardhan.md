# Dr. Sunita Patwardhan, Gynaecology

## Profile

Dr. Sunita Patwardhan is a consultant in the Gynaecology department (third floor). OPD days: Monday, Tuesday, Thursday and Friday, 09:30–13:30. Languages: Marathi, English and Hindi. Each appointment slot is 20 minutes.

## Areas of interest

pregnancy care and high-risk pregnancies.

## How to book

Book, reschedule or cancel an appointment by chatting with CareFlow in English, Hindi or Marathi, or at the reception desk on the ground floor. Walk-in patients get a token at the reception and are seen between booked appointments.
