# Dr. Snehal Gokhale, ENT (ear, nose and throat)

## Profile

Dr. Snehal Gokhale is a consultant in the ENT (ear, nose and throat) department (second floor). OPD days: Monday, Wednesday and Friday, 09:00–13:00. Languages: Marathi and English. Each appointment slot is 15 minutes.

## Areas of interest

sinus disease, allergies and children's ENT problems.

## How to book

Book, reschedule or cancel an appointment by chatting with CareFlow in English, Hindi or Marathi, or at the reception desk on the ground floor. Walk-in patients get a token at the reception and are seen between booked appointments.
