# Dr. Lakshmi Rao, Ophthalmology (eye care)

## Profile

Dr. Lakshmi Rao is a consultant in the Ophthalmology (eye care) department (fourth floor). OPD days: Tuesday, Thursday and Saturday, 09:00–13:00. Languages: English. Each appointment slot is 10 minutes.

## Areas of interest

glaucoma and children's eye care.

## How to book

Book, reschedule or cancel an appointment by chatting with CareFlow in English, Hindi or Marathi, or at the reception desk on the ground floor. Walk-in patients get a token at the reception and are seen between booked appointments.
