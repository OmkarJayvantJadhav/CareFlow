# Dr. Imran Shaikh, General Medicine

## Profile

Dr. Imran Shaikh is a consultant in the General Medicine department (first floor). OPD days: Tuesday, Thursday and Saturday, 09:00–14:00. Languages: Hindi and English. Each appointment slot is 10 minutes.

## Areas of interest

infections, asthma and elderly care.

## How to book

Book, reschedule or cancel an appointment by chatting with CareFlow in English, Hindi or Marathi, or at the reception desk on the ground floor. Walk-in patients get a token at the reception and are seen between booked appointments.
