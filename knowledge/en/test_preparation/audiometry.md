# Hearing test (audiometry): preparation

## Overview

Audiometry measures how well you hear sounds of different pitch and loudness. It is done in the ENT department. The test itself takes about 30 minutes.

## Before the test

- No fasting is needed (you do not need an empty stomach); you can eat and drink normally.
- Avoid loud noise such as concerts, firecrackers or loud music for 12 hours before the test.
- Bring the doctor's prescription or test request, a photo ID and any previous reports.

## On the day of the test

The test is done in the department's clinic room during OPD hours; ask the reception for the room number.

## Reports

The result is usually explained to you the same day by the doctor. Collect printed reports at the Reports counter on the ground floor (bring the receipt), or open the secure link sent by SMS.
