# Iron studies (serum iron, ferritin): preparation

## Overview

Iron studies check the amount of iron in the blood and the body's iron stores. The test itself takes about 5 minutes.

## Before the test

- Fasting is required (come on an empty stomach): do not eat or drink anything except plain water for 8 hours before the test.
- Plain water is allowed during the fast; tea, coffee, milk and juice are not.
- Come in the morning, ideally between 07:00 and 10:00, so the fasting period is easy to manage.
- Bring the doctor's prescription or test request, a photo ID and any previous reports.

## On the day of the test

Samples are collected at the Laboratory on the ground floor, Monday to Saturday 07:00–14:00 and Sunday 08:00–11:00. No appointment is needed.

## Reports

Reports are usually ready the next working day. Collect printed reports at the Reports counter on the ground floor (bring the receipt), or open the secure link sent by SMS.
