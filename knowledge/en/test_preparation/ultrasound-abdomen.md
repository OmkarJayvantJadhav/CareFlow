# Ultrasound of the upper abdomen: preparation

## Overview

This scan uses sound waves to look at the liver, gall bladder, pancreas and kidneys. The test itself takes about 20 minutes.

## Before the test

- Fasting is required (come on an empty stomach): do not eat or drink anything except plain water for 6 hours before the test.
- Plain water is allowed during the fast; tea, coffee, milk and juice are not.
- Wear loose, comfortable clothes without metal buttons or zips.
- Bring the doctor's prescription or test request, a photo ID and any previous reports.

## On the day of the test

The test is done in the Radiology department on the first floor, Monday to Saturday 08:00–20:00. Book a time at the Radiology desk or the reception, and arrive 15 minutes early.

## Reports

Reports are usually ready the same day after 18:00. Collect printed reports at the Reports counter on the ground floor (bring the receipt), or open the secure link sent by SMS.
