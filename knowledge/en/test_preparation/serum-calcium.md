# Serum calcium: preparation

## Overview

This test measures the calcium level in the blood. The test itself takes about 5 minutes.

## Before the test

- No fasting is needed (you do not need an empty stomach); you can eat and drink normally.
- Bring the doctor's prescription or test request, a photo ID and any previous reports.

## On the day of the test

Samples are collected at the Laboratory on the ground floor, Monday to Saturday 07:00–14:00 and Sunday 08:00–11:00. No appointment is needed.

## Reports

Reports are usually ready the same day after 18:00. Collect printed reports at the Reports counter on the ground floor (bring the receipt), or open the secure link sent by SMS.
