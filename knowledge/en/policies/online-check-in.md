# Checking in for your appointment

## When you can check in

You can check in yourself from {{check_in_opens_minutes}} minutes before your appointment time until {{late_grace_minutes}} minutes after it starts. After that, please check in at the reception desk; you are then seen in order of arrival. A caregiver with a verified link can check in for the patient.

## How to check in

- Ask the CareFlow assistant to check you in, or use 'Check in now' under My appointments.
- Scan the QR code on your receipt at the self check-in kiosk at the reception.
- Or show your receipt code at the reception desk.

## After checking in

You get a token straight away and can follow your place and estimated wait on the Queue status page.
