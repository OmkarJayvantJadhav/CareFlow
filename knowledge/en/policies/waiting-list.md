# Waiting list

## Joining the waiting list

If a doctor has no free slot on the dates you want, CareFlow can add you to that doctor's waiting list for those dates, after you agree. You can be on each doctor's list only once.

## When a slot opens

When someone cancels or moves an appointment, the slot is offered first to the patient who joined the list earliest for that date. You get a message, and the slot is held for you for up to 2 hours. If you do not book it in time, it is offered to the next patient.

## Leaving the list

If you book any appointment with that doctor, your waiting-list entry for the doctor is closed automatically.
