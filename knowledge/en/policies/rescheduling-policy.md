# Rescheduling policy

## When you can reschedule

Appointments can be rescheduled up to 12 hours before the appointment time, to any free slot in the same department. Within 12 hours of the appointment, it can no longer be moved online; please contact the reception desk.

## What happens to the old slot

When you move an appointment, your old slot is released and offered first to patients on the doctor's waiting list.

## If the doctor becomes unavailable

If the hospital cancels your appointment because the doctor is unavailable, you can reschedule free of charge at any time, even within 12 hours, to any doctor in the same department.
