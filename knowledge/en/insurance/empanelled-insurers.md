# Empanelled insurers and TPAs

## Insurers

The hospital offers cashless treatment for policies from Kavach Health Insurance, Sanjeevani General Insurance and Nirbhay Assurance.

## Third-party administrators (TPAs)

Claims are also accepted through MediLink TPA and CareBridge TPA. If your insurer is not listed, you can pay and claim reimbursement later. All insurer, TPA and company names in this document are fictional.
