# OPD hours by department

## Hours

- General Medicine: 09:00–14:00
- Cardiology: 09:00–13:00
- ENT (ear, nose and throat): 09:00–13:00
- Orthopaedics: 09:30–13:30
- Paediatrics: 10:00–14:00
- Dermatology: 09:00–13:00
- Gynaecology: 09:30–13:30
- Ophthalmology (eye care): 09:00–13:00

## Days

OPDs run Monday to Saturday on each doctor's OPD days. On Sundays only the emergency department, pharmacy and a short laboratory session (08:00–11:00) are open.
