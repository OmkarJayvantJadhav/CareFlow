# Floor guide

## Where to find departments

- Ground floor: reception and help desk, emergency department, laboratory, pharmacy and billing, reports counter, insurance desk and medical records.
- First floor: General Medicine, Paediatrics, radiology (X-ray, ultrasound, CT, MRI).
- Second floor: ENT (ear, nose and throat), Orthopaedics, day-care procedure suite.
- Third floor: Cardiology, Gynaecology.
- Fourth floor: Dermatology, Ophthalmology (eye care), canteen.

## Lifts and stairs

Four lifts are next to the reception; the lift on the left is reserved for patients on stretchers and wheelchairs.
